# D3 Impact Case & Cost-Value Analysis

## The Opportunity: Closing Information Gaps
A large share of sessions end in browsing at the final step (the betslip confirm button). Our thesis is that users abandoning the flow at this stage face an **information gap**, not a motivation gap. 

By accurately diagnosing their hesitation (e.g., "Did the odds change?") and providing a targeted, factual resolution, we prevent unnecessary abandonment.

## Value Proposition
*   **Target Uplift:** We target a **+10% uplift in session-to-action conversion** for hesitated sessions.
*   **Cost Avoidance:** Replacing aggressive push tactics with contextual clarity reduces customer churn and regulatory fines.
*   **User Retention:** A confident user is a retained user. Providing clarifications builds trust over long-term platform usage.

## The Session Quality Score (SQS)
Our primary optimization metric is not raw conversion, but the **Session Quality Score**:
`SQS = w1(Action Value) + w2(Discovery Efficiency) - w3(Harm Indicator Load)`

Because `w3 >= w1 + w2`, any short-term conversion gain achieved through harmful play is mathematically penalized into a negative score. This perfectly aligns FEG's business goals with player safety.
